WHY SERAI

Where the caravans stopped for the night

On the Silk Road, travellers rested in caravanserais: walled inns about a day’s ride apart, built around one courtyard behind a single gate. Merchants from Xi’an, Samarkand, Tabriz and Venice slept under the same roof, traded silk and spices, and picked up each other’s words to close a deal.

Serai works the same way. Every board is a stop on the road, and the words you find are what you carry on to the next one. Fifteen languages share one courtyard, and you can travel from any of them into any other.

Questions and answers

What is Serai?
Serai is a word-search game for learning vocabulary on iPhone. You read a word in your own language, then find its translation on a board of letters by swiping through it. Words snake around corners, and every letter belongs to exactly one word.
Which languages can I learn with Serai?
Serai has fifteen languages: English, German, Russian, French, Kazakh, Czech, Spanish, Chinese (Simplified, with pinyin), Korean, Italian, Mongolian, Turkish, Japanese, Portuguese and Ukrainian. You can learn from any of them into any other. The app itself is also available in these fifteen languages.
How many words and which levels does Serai cover?
About 8,000 words from beginner to mastery, following the CEFR levels A1 to C2 and grouped into everyday topics. A three-minute placement test finds your starting level, and spaced review brings each word back just before you would forget it.
Is Serai free?
Yes. Serai is free to download, with all languages, levels, the Daily Hunt and review included, and without ads or a subscription. For players who want more there are four optional expeditions, each a one-time purchase: The Taklamakan Crossing (€9.99) adds description clues, The Jade Gate adds opposite boards, where you find each word from its opposite, The Pamir Pass (€4.99) adds word ladders, where each word is one letter from the next, and The Mogao Cave (€4.99) adds flashcards that feed the same spaced review as the boards.
Do I need an account or an internet connection?
No. Serai works without an account and offline. Your progress is stored only on your iPhone; the app does not collect personal data or track you.
Which devices does Serai run on?
Serai is made for iPhone and requires iOS 15 or later. It is coming soon to the App Store.
